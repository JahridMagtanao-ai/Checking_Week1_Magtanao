About Pickleball

Pickleball is a paddle sport that mixes ideas from tennis, badminton, and table tennis. Players use a solid paddle to hit a light plastic ball with holes over a low net. It can be played as singles (one player per side) or doubles (two players per side).

The sport was invented in the United States in 1965 and is now played all over the world. It is popular with students because it is easy to learn, needs a small court, and is fun with friends of any skill level.

Why Try Pickleball?

Easy to Learn

The rules are simple, so most beginners can play a real game on day one.

Good Exercise

It keeps you moving and builds balance and coordination without being too hard.

Social and Fun

Games are quick and friendly, which makes it a great way to meet people.
