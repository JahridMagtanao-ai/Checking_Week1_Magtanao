Basic Rules of Pickleball

Start with these seven rules and learn the rest as you play.

  1. The Court

    20 feet wide and 44 feet long, about the size of a badminton court, split by a low net.

  2. The Serve

    Serve underhand, below the waist, from behind the back line to the diagonal service area.

  3. Two-Bounce Rule

    After the serve, the ball must bounce once on each side before anyone can hit it in the air.

  4. The Kitchen

    No volleys while standing in the 7-foot zone next to the net. Step in only after the ball bounces.

  5. Scoring

    Only the serving side scores. Games go to 11 points and you must win by 2.

  6. Faults

    The rally ends if the ball goes out, hits the net, is volleyed in the kitchen, or bounces twice on your side.

  7. Fair Play

    Call balls honestly. If you are unsure, give the point to your opponent.

  8. Equipment

    A paddle, a light plastic ball with holes, non-marking sports shoes, and water.
