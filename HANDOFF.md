**Project Title:** Pickleball Fun: A Simple Website for Pickleball Beginners

---

## UX Project Proposal

**User Problem**
Some people do not know what pickleball is, how to play it, or what equipment they need.

**Target Audience**
Students, beginners, and people interested in sports.

**Website Goal**
To help beginners understand pickleball and learn the basic things they need to start playing.

**Design Theme**
- Colors: green, white, and yellow
- Style: simple, clean, and sporty
- Font: Arial

---

## Website Pages

| Page | File | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `index.html` | Welcome page with a pickleball picture |
| About | `about.html` | Short introduction to pickleball |
| Rules | `rules.html` | Basic rules and equipment |
| Gallery | `gallery.html` | Pictures of pickleball |
| Ask Us | `ask.html` | Ask a question or send a request |

All pages share the same menu at the top and the same stylesheet.

---

## Design Tokens

Declared in the `:root` block of `css/style.css`:

- **Color:** `--color-primary` (green), `--color-bg` (white), `--color-accent` (yellow)
- **Typography:** `--font-family` (Arial) and a size scale from `--fs-sm` to `--fs-2xl`
- **Layout:** `--container-max` and a spacing scale from `--space-1` to `--space-6`
